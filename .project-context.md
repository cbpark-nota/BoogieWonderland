## Core Algorithm (v3, 균형형)
Entry: ADX≥25 AND 20MA>50MA>200MA AND RSI∈[50,75] AND no vol_spike>3x(20d) AND no ±10%(5d) AND HH-HL≥3(60d) AND price≥80% of 52w high
Score = ADX×0.4 + 3M_Return×0.3 + Sector_Strength×0.2 + Vol_Stability×0.1
Stop = 20d_High - ATR(14)×2.5
Position: score_capped, max 20%/stock, top 10, biweekly rebalance
Market filter: SPY golden cross (50MA>200MA)
